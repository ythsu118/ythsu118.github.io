Social connectedness, spirituality & mental health

Epidemiological work presented at APA, APHA, SER, and AAGP annual meetings

This line of work uses longitudinal, population-based data to examine how social connectedness, loneliness, social isolation, and religion and spirituality shape mental health and substance use among midlife and older adults, with a focus on Black Americans.
